Laundry Solutions for Apartments in St. George Utah

Published April 4, 2026 · Updated July 27, 2026

Most apartments in St. George have washer and dryer hookups. Most do not include the machines. You move in, find two empty hookups in a closet, and figure it out from there.

St. George is one of the fastest-growing cities in Utah. New apartment complexes are going up across the city, from Little Valley to SunRiver to the Desert Color area. Washington County added over 10,000 new housing units between 2020 and 2025. A large share of those are apartments and townhomes built for renters.

Every one of those renters needs a plan for laundry. There are three main options: community laundry rooms, laundromats, or your own machines inside your apartment. Each comes with a different cost, different level of convenience, and different headaches.

Community Laundry Rooms in St. George Apartments

Many apartment complexes offer a shared laundry room on-site. These rooms have coin-operated or card-operated washers and dryers. A single wash cycle costs $2 to $3. A dryer cycle costs $1.50 to $2.50. One full load runs $3.50 to $5.50 total.

The average household does 5 to 7 loads of laundry per week. At the low end, that is $70 per month in quarters. At the high end, $154 per month. The machines are shared. You carry your laundry down the hall or across the parking lot. You wait for open machines. Peak times on weekday evenings and weekends mean longer waits.

Late-night laundry is often not an option. Many complex laundry rooms close at 9 or 10 PM. If you work evening shifts, your schedule does not align with the laundry room hours.

Shared machines also wear out faster. Heavy use means more breakdowns. When a machine is down, the remaining machines get even more traffic. Hygiene is a concern for some tenants. You do not know what the person before you washed in that machine.

Hauling Laundry to a Laundromat

St. George has several laundromats. The cost per load is similar to apartment laundry rooms, around $3 to $5 per load. You also spend time and gas driving there.

A typical laundromat trip takes 1 to 2 hours. That includes loading the car, driving, waiting for machines, folding, and driving home. If you go once a week, that is 4 to 8 hours per month spent on laundry logistics. At two trips per week, double it.

Gas costs in St. George average $3.50 to $4.00 per gallon. A 10-minute round trip to the nearest laundromat adds $2 to $4 per visit in fuel. Over a month, that is another $8 to $16 on top of the machine costs.

Laundromats work in a pinch. They are not a long-term solution for anyone who values their time.

Buying a Washer and Dryer for Your Apartment

Buying your own machines solves the convenience problem. You do laundry whenever you want. No shared machines. No quarters. No trips across town.

The problem is cost and commitment. A mid-range washer and dryer set costs $1,200 to $1,800. Delivery runs $50 to $150. Installation is $100 to $200. Sales tax in St. George adds 6.75%. Total upfront cost: $1,450 to $2,300.

That is a big investment for an apartment you rent. If your lease is 12 months, you spent $1,500 or more on machines you need to move or sell when the lease ends. Used washers and dryers sell for 30 to 50 cents on the dollar. You lose $750 to $1,000 on resale.

Moving the machines is expensive too. Hiring movers to transport a washer and dryer costs $150 to $300. If your next apartment has different hookup configurations, you have another problem.

Some landlords prefer tenants not buy cheap machines. Low-end washers are more likely to leak or malfunction. A leaking washer in an upstairs apartment causes water damage to the unit below. Landlords end up paying for repairs that a tenant's bargain washer caused.

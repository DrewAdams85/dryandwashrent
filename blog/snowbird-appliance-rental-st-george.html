Why Snowbirds in St. George Rent Appliances Instead of Buying

Published April 4, 2026 · Updated July 27, 2026

Buying a washer and dryer for a place you live in 5 months a year is a bad deal. Thousands of snowbirds in St. George have figured this out. They rent appliances for the season and return them when they head north.

St. George is one of the top snowbird destinations in the western United States. Winter temperatures sit between 55 and 60 degrees. The red rock scenery is world-class. The cost of living is lower than Phoenix or Palm Springs. Seasonal residents start arriving in October and leave by April.

Most of them need laundry machines. Most of their rentals do not come with them.

The Problem with Buying Appliances for Half a Year

A mid-range washer and dryer set costs $1,200 to $1,800. A decent pair runs about $1,500. That is a lot of money for machines you will use 4 to 6 months out of 12.

When you leave in spring, those machines sit idle. They collect dust. Seals dry out. Hoses can crack. Residual moisture inside a dormant washer breeds mold and mildew. A machine that sat unused for 6 months often needs cleaning, troubleshooting, or repair before it runs properly again.

Appliances also depreciate whether you use them or not. A $1,500 set loses 15 to 20 percent of its value per year. After 3 seasons, your machines are worth $600 to $800 on the resale market. You paid $1,500.

There is also the question of what to do with them. If you own your winter home, they sit in a vacant house. If you rent seasonally, you need to sell them, store them, or haul them back north. None of those options is cheap or easy.

How Much Does a Laundromat Cost in 2026? Average Prices Per Load

Published April 4, 2026 · Updated September 17, 2026

The average laundromat costs $4.00 to $8.00 per load in 2026. That is $2.00 to $5.00 to wash and $1.50 to $4.00 to dry. A single person spends about $40 to $65 per month. A family of three or four spends $100 to $150 before gas and detergent.

Here is the full breakdown: average laundromat prices by machine size, what the dryers cost, how much money to bring, and what laundry costs per month across the US. It also covers prices here in St. George, Utah, and when having machines at home beats the laundromat.

Laundromat Cost Per Load in 2026

A standard wash cycle runs $2.00 to $5.00. The price depends on the machine size. A regular top-loader sits around $2.00 to $3.00. A double-load front-loader costs $3.50 to $5.00, and large-capacity machines for comforters and family loads run $6.00 to $10.00. Prices skew higher in big cities, where a single load can reach $10.

Drying adds $1.50 to $4.00 per load. Most dryers charge about $0.25 for every 5 to 8 minutes, and a full load takes 30 to 45 minutes. Thick items like towels and blankets often need a second round.

One load of laundry costs $4.00 to $8.00 when you combine washing and drying. That is the baseline. Detergent from the vending machine adds another $1.00 to $2.00 if you forget to bring your own.

Coin laundries, washaterias, and laundrettes all price the same way. The name changes by region. The cost per load does not.

Average Laundromat Prices by Machine Size

Laundromats price washers by capacity. The bigger the drum, the higher the price per cycle.

Machine Holds Average Price

Triple-load front-loader 3 loads (30 lb) $5.00 – $6.50
Large washer 4 loads (40 lb) $6.00 – $8.00
Mega washer 6 to 8 loads (60–80 lb) $8.00 – $10.00+
Dryer 30 to 45 minutes $1.50 – $4.00

Bigger machines cost more per cycle and less per pound. Four separate top-load washes cost $8.00 to $12.00. One 40-pound machine washes the same pile for $6.00 to $8.00.

Use the big machines for bedding, towels, and a full week of family laundry. Use the small ones for a single basket.

How Much Does It Cost to Dry Clothes at a Laundromat?

A laundromat dryer costs $1.50 to $4.00 per load. Most charge $0.25 for every 5 to 8 minutes. The final price depends on how long your clothes take to dry.

Load Dry Time Dryer Cost
Light load (shirts, synthetics) 20 – 30 minutes $1.00 – $1.50
Full mixed load 30 – 45 minutes $1.50 – $2.50
Towels, jeans, bedding 45 – 60 minutes $2.50 – $4.00

The dryer is where people overpay. Three habits cut the bill:

  • Pick the highest spin speed on the washer. Less water in the clothes means fewer minutes in the dryer.
  • Do not overload the dryer. A packed drum takes twice as long and costs twice as much.
  • Combine two washer loads into one large dryer. Most laundromat dryers hold 30 to 45 pounds.

How Much Money Should You Bring to the Laundromat?

Bring $10 for one load and $20 for two. That covers the wash, the dry, a second dry cycle for thick items, and detergent from the vending machine.

Check how the laundromat takes payment before you go. Coin laundries run on quarters, and one load takes 16 to 32 of them. Newer laundromats use a reloadable card or a phone app. Change machines break, so bring your own quarters to a coin laundry.

How Much Does Laundry Cost Per Month?

A single person doing 1 to 2 loads per week spends $16 to $64 per month at the machines. Most land around $40 to $65 once detergent and the occasional double-dry cycle are counted.

A typical family of 3 to 4 people generates 5 to 8 loads of laundry per week. That is 20 to 32 loads per month. At the low end, 20 loads at $4.00 each costs $80 per month. At the high end, 32 loads at $5.50 each costs $176 per month. Most families land between $100 and $150 per month on laundromat costs alone.

How Much Does Wash-and-Fold Laundry Service Cost?

Full-service wash-and-fold is the expensive shortcut. Drop-off laundry service runs $1.50 to $3.00 per pound, so a typical 15-pound bag costs $25 to $45. Doing that weekly puts a family well past $200 per month.

Pickup and delivery services charge more. Expect $2.00 to $3.50 per pound plus a delivery fee or a minimum order of $30 to $40.

Laundromat vs. Laundry at Home: Cost Per Load

A load of laundry at home costs $1.00 to $2.00. Electricity for the washer and dryer runs $0.60 to $1.00. Water adds $0.15 to $0.30. Detergent adds $0.25 to $0.50.

The same load costs $4.00 to $8.00 at the laundromat. At 25 loads a month, that gap is $75 to $150.

The Hidden Costs Most People Ignore

Gas and Drive Time

Unless you live next door to one, the nearest laundromat is a 5 to 15 minute drive. A round trip twice a week burns 2 to 3 gallons of gas per month. At current gas prices, that adds $7 to $10 monthly.

Time Spent at the Laundromat

Each laundromat trip takes 1 to 2 hours. You have to load machines, wait for cycles, move clothes to the dryer, fold, and drive home. Two trips per week means 8 to 16 hours per month sitting in a laundromat.

That is 2 to 4 full workdays every month. For a family with jobs and kids, those hours have real value. You could be working, cooking, or actually enjoying your weekend instead.

Wear on Your Clothes

Commercial laundromat machines run hotter and more aggressively than home units. Clothes fade faster. Elastic breaks down sooner. You replace items more often. It is hard to put a dollar amount on this, but it adds up over a year.

The Real Cost of Using a Laundromat in St. George Utah

April 4, 2026

A single laundromat trip in St. George costs more than you think. The per-load price is only the start. You also pay for gas, time, and the hassle of hauling baskets across town.

I tracked real laundromat costs in St. George over several months. The numbers make a strong case for renting a washer and dryer at home instead.

What a Laundromat Costs Per Load in St. George

A standard wash cycle at a St. George laundromat runs $2.50 to $5.00. The price depends on the machine size. A regular top-loader sits around $2.50. A large-capacity front-loader costs $4.00 to $5.00.

Drying adds $1.50 to $3.00 per load. Most dryers need 2 to 3 quarters for a full cycle. Thick items like towels and blankets often need a second round.

One load of laundry costs $4.00 to $8.00 when you combine washing and drying. That is the baseline. Detergent from the vending machine adds another $1.00 to $2.00 if you forget to bring your own.

Monthly Laundromat Cost for a Family

A typical family of 3 to 4 people generates 5 to 8 loads of laundry per week. That is 20 to 32 loads per month.

At the low end, 20 loads at $4.00 each costs $80 per month. At the high end, 32 loads at $5.50 each costs $176 per month. Most families land between $100 and $150 per month on laundromat costs alone.

That number does not include gas, detergent you buy separately, or the value of your time.

The Hidden Costs Most People Ignore

Gas and Drive Time

St. George is spread out. Depending on where you live, the nearest laundromat is a 5 to 15 minute drive. A round trip twice a week burns 2 to 3 gallons of gas per month. At current Utah gas prices, that adds $7 to $10 monthly.

Time Spent at the Laundromat

Each laundromat trip takes 1 to 2 hours. You have to load machines, wait for cycles, move clothes to the dryer, fold, and drive home. Two trips per week means 8 to 16 hours per month sitting in a laundromat.

That is 2 to 4 full workdays every month. For a family with jobs and kids, those hours have real value. You could be working, cooking, or spending time at Snow Canyon instead.

Wear on Your Clothes

Commercial laundromat machines run hotter and more aggressively than home units. Clothes fade faster. Elastic breaks down sooner. You replace items more often. It is hard to put a dollar amount on this, but it adds up over a year.

St. George Laundromat Options Are Shrinking

St. George has grown from 80,000 to over 100,000 residents in the past few years. The city keeps expanding south and west. New housing developments in Little Valley, Desert Color, and along the Southern Parkway are miles from the nearest laundromat.

Laundromats have not kept pace with the population growth. Fewer locations mean longer drives, more crowded machines, and less availability on weekends. Saturday mornings at any St. George laundromat now means waiting for an open machine.

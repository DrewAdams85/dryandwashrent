# 90-Day Growth Roadmap — DryAndWashRent

Practical ideas the new owner can work through over their first 3 months. Organized into **Website Improvements** and a **Social Media Plan**. Pick what fits your bandwidth — none of this is mandatory, but the items marked ⭐ are the highest leverage for a local rental business.

Audience to keep in mind:
- **Snowbirds** wintering in St. George / Mesquite (Oct–April)
- **College students** (Utah Tech / Dixie State, fall semester start)
- **Apartment renters** without hookups or with old machines
- **Short-term housing residents** (traveling nurses, contractors, Airbnb hosts)
- **Locals priced out of buying** a new washer/dryer set

# Part 1 — Website Improvements

## Month 1: Foundation & quick wins

### ⭐ Google Business Profile (biggest gap for any local business)
- [ ] Claim / verify https://business.google.com for "DryAndWashRent" — St. George service area.
- [ ] Add service area: St. George, Washington, Hurricane, Ivins, Santa Clara, Mesquite NV.
- [ ] Upload 10+ photos: clean machines, install in progress, delivery truck, team.
- [ ] Add service categories: Appliance rental service, Appliance store.
- [ ] List services + prices ($60/mo bundle, $39.99 install).
- [ ] Post weekly updates (offers, tips, customer wins) — these show in Maps results.

### ⭐ Reviews engine
- [ ] After every successful install, text/email the customer a direct Google review link.
- [ ] Goal: 25 reviews in 90 days. This single thing outranks most SEO work for local searches.
- [ ] Respond to every review within 48 hours, good or bad.

### Site fixes
- [ ] Add a **"Service Areas"** page listing every city served, each with its own short paragraph. Helps rank for "washer dryer rental [city]".
- [ ] Add a **FAQ schema** to the homepage so questions appear directly in Google search results.
- [ ] Add a **"How it works"** section with 3 simple steps (Order → We deliver & install → Pay monthly).
- [ ] Add **before/after photos** from real installs (with permission).
- [ ] Add a **sticky "Call now" button** on mobile that's visible while scrolling.

### Conversion tracking
- [ ] In GA4, mark "phone tap" (`tel:` link click), "SMS tap", and "form submit" as **conversions**. You can't improve what you don't measure.
- [ ] Set up a weekly email report in GA4 showing top traffic sources + conversions.

# Part 2 — Social Media Plan

For a local rental business, social media is a **trust + visibility play**, not a sales channel. People won't usually rent a washer from a TikTok — but they'll Google you after seeing your post and convert from the website.

## Where to focus (and where not to)

| Platform | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | ⭐ High | St. George demographics skew older; Facebook Marketplace + community groups are huge here |
| **Instagram** | Medium | Good for visuals, younger renters, snowbird kids researching for parents |
| **Nextdoor** | ⭐ High | Hyper-local; the place neighbors ask for recommendations |
| **TikTok** | Low–Medium | Only if you have time to film; reaches college students |
| **YouTube** | Medium | Great for evergreen content (search-driven, not feed-driven) |
| **Twitter/X** | Skip | Not where your customers are |
| **LinkedIn** | Skip | Not relevant for B2C rental |

## 12-week content calendar (template)

### Weeks 1–4 — "We exist" phase
Goal: introduce the business locally.

- Meet the owner video (Instagram + Facebook)
- "Why we started DryAndWashRent" post
- Photo tour of a typical install (start to finish)
- "5 St. George apartments without W/D hookups — and the fix"
- Customer #1 testimonial post
- Reel: "30 seconds — how delivery works"
- Facebook ad ($50 budget) targeting St. George zip codes

### Weeks 5–8 — "We help" phase
Goal: prove value with tips.

- "How often should you really clean your dryer vent?" (carousel)
- "Snowbird tip: arriving in October? Book now"
- Behind-the-scenes: cleaning + sanitizing returned machines
- College student feature: "Move-in laundry hack for Utah Tech students"
- Before/after of a tight apartment install
- YouTube: "How to load a washer correctly" (basic but searched a lot)
- Customer review screenshot post

### Weeks 9–12 — "We're trusted" phase
Goal: ramp social proof, drive referrals.

- "100 installs done!" milestone post (whatever the real number is)
- Customer wall: 6 short testimonials in one carousel
- Partnership announcement (property manager / realtor / Airbnb host)
- Holiday-tied offer: "December special — first month $30 off"
- "Year in review" infographic
- Reel: "5 signs your apartment's washer is about to die"
- Run a giveaway: "Tag a friend who hates laundromats — winner gets first month free"

---

## Content pillars (rotate these)

Every post should fit one of five buckets. This keeps your feed varied without you having to invent ideas each week.

1. **Customer stories** — photos, quotes, before/after.
2. **Behind the scenes** — installs, cleaning, the team.
3. **Helpful tips** — laundry, appliance maintenance, saving money.
4. **Local angle** — St. George news, events, neighborhoods, seasons.
5. **Offers / CTAs** — pricing, referral codes, limited-time deals.

Rule of thumb: **4 helpful posts for every 1 sales post.** People unfollow accounts that only sell.

---

## Facebook groups + Nextdoor strategy

These are where local rentals actually get booked. Don't spam — be a helpful neighbor first.

- [ ] Join St. George / Washington County / Mesquite community groups.
- [ ] Join Utah Tech parent / student groups.
- [ ] When someone asks "where can I rent a washer/dryer?" — reply with a short, friendly recommendation (not a copy-paste ad).
- [ ] Most groups allow **one self-promo post per week or month** — read the rules.
- [ ] Nextdoor: post a useful tip once a week (not an ad). Your business name shows on every post — that's the marketing.

---

## Paid ads (only if budget allows)

Start small. **$100/month is enough to learn.**

- [ ] **Facebook/Instagram ads** — target zip codes for St. George + Mesquite, age 25–65, interests: renting, moving, apartment living.
- [ ] **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, shows at the top of local searches. Excellent for service businesses.
- [ ] **Google Search Ads** — bid on "washer dryer rental st george" and variations. Even 5–10 clicks a day is meaningful.

Track every lead source. Ask every caller "How did you hear about us?" and log it.
